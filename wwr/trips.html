Featured Rafting Packages

Tana River Half-Day Run

An ideal introduction to white water rafting for beginners and families. Enjoy scenic water views, gentle Class II–III rapid sections, and expert guide instruction throughout the trip.

Grand Naivasha Springs

Designed for thrill-seekers looking for maximum adrenaline. Navigate high-energy Class IV rapids through steep rock gorges, sharp turns, and powerful river drops.

Masai Mara Wilderness Safari

Combine thrilling daytime whitewater action with a relaxing evening under the stars. Includes a full-day guided river tour, campfire dining, and riverside tent accommodations.
